his FAQ provides updated information on the Puja Permission Portal and includes additional details specific to the process of applying for permissions.

The Puja Permission Portal is an online platform where Puja committees can apply for the necessary permissions from various government departments to organize Durga Puja and other festivals.

Any registered Puja committee or club that had Puja permission last year can apply for permission through the portal.

  • To apply for Puja permission, follow these steps:
  • 1. Register your committee on the portal.
  • 2. Log in with your credentials.
  • 3. Fill out the application form with the required details.
  • 4. Upload the necessary documents.
  • 5. Submit the application.

    Typically, you will need the following documents:
  • 1. Last year's Puja Permission
  • 2. NOC from the landowner
  • 3.Electricity bill
  • 4.Electrician’s declaration
  • 5. Decorator’s declaration
  • 6.Site Plan
  • 7.Secretary/President’s Signature

No, once you have submitted the application, you cannot modify it. Please review all the details carefully before the final submission.

You will receive a notification on the portal as well as an email/SMS alert once your application is approved or if further clarification is required.

The processing time may vary depending on the department. Typically, it can take between 10-20 business days. Keep checking your application status on the portal.

If your application is objected, the reasons will be mentioned in the objected note. You can correct the mentioned issues and resubmit.

For any technical assistance or queries, you can contact the support team via the WhatsApp number provided on the portal.

Yes, there will be a deadline for submitting Puja permission application. Please ensure that you apply within the specified time frame.

Once your application is approved, you can log in to the portal and download the permission letter from your dashboard.
