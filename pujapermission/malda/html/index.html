Welcome to Malda Puja Permission

Experience Tradition, Devotion & Celebration

Malda district, with its deep-rooted cultural and religious traditions, celebrates Durga Puja and other festivals with grandeur and devotion. From English Bazar to Chanchal, every town and village comes alive with artistic pandals, traditional rituals, and enthusiastic community participation. Malda’s pujas reflect the harmony of old-world charm and modern creativity, making it one of the most vibrant festive regions of North Bengal.

Durga puja

The biggest festival in Malda, marked by grand processions, artistic themes, and huge crowds enjoying the five days of celebration.

Kali Puja

Celebrated during Diwali, Kali Puja in Malda is known for its spiritual fervor, firecrackers, and late-night rituals.

Jagadhatri Puja

Though less widespread, it is observed in parts of Malda with beautiful idols and peaceful ceremonies.

The Puja Permission Portal is an online platform where Puja committees can apply for the necessary permissions from various government departments to organize Durga Puja and other festivals.

Any registered Puja committee or club that had Puja permission last year can apply for permission through the portal.

    To apply for Puja permission, follow these steps:
  • 1. Register your committee on the portal.
  • 2. Log in with your credentials.
  • 3. Fill out the application form with the required details.
  • 4. Upload the necessary documents.
  • 5. Submit the application.

    Typically, you will need the following documents:
  • 1. Last year's Puja Permission
  • 2. NOC from the landowner
  • 3.Electricity bill
  • 4.Electrician’s declaration
  • 5. Decorator’s declaration
  • 6.Site Plan
  • 7.Secretary/President’s Signature
