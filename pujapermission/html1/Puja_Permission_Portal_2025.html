About Us

Welcome to Online Puja Permission Portal

Durga Puja, the grand annual festival celebrated predominantly in West Bengal, was inscribed by UNESCO in 2021 on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. It is not only a religious event but also a vibrant cultural celebration that brings together art, music, craft, performance, and community participation. The festival transforms the city into a massive public art gallery with elaborate pandals and idols, reflecting Bengal’s deep-rooted artistic traditions and collective spirit. UNESCO recognized Durga Puja for its inclusive, artistic, and celebratory character that transcends religion and unites people from all walks of life.

Frequently Asked Questions

The FAQs for the Puja Permission Portal:

  • What is the Puja Permission Portal?

    The Puja Permission Portal is an online platform designed to simplify the process of securing all necessary approvals from various government departments in Bankura for organizing Durga Puja and other festivals.

  • Who can apply for Puja permission?

    Registered Puja committees or clubs that successfully hosted Durga Puja last year can apply for this year’s permissions through the portal.

  • How do I apply for Puja permission online?

    Applying for Puja permission is simple:

    1. Register your committee on the portal.

    2.Log in using your credentials.

    3.Complete the application form with all required information.

    4.Upload the necessary documents.

    5.Hit the submit button, and you’re done
