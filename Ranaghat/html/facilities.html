Facilities in Ranaghat

The town offers a range of government-supported facilities that ensure education, healthcare, and social welfare reach every citizen.

🎓 Educational Facilities (Government)

Ranaghat is home to several reputed government and government-aided institutions that provide quality education from primary to college level.

1. Ranaghat High School

A historic boys’ institution known for academic discipline.

Ranaghat Girls’ High School

Empowering girls through free and accessible education.

Ranaghat Ramlal Academy

A well-known government-aided school.

Ranaghat College

Affiliated with the University of Kalyani, this government-aided college offers undergraduate degrees in Arts, Science, and Commerce.

🏥 Healthcare Facilities (Government)

Ranaghat provides essential medical services through government-run hospitals and health centers.

1. Ranaghat Sub-Divisional Hospital

The town’s main public hospital, offering emergency care, maternity services, surgery, and outpatient departments.

Primary Health Centres (PHCs)

Located in surrounding areas like Habibpur and Nokari to ensure rural health coverage.

Urban Health Units & AYUSH Clinics

Additional support in homeopathy, Ayurveda, and alternative medicine.
