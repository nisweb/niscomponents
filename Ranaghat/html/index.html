About Us

Welcome to
SDO Ranaghat

Ranaghat Sub-division, at the time of its constitution, during the British Raj, was bounded on the West, by the Hoogly river, on the South by the Barasat subdivision of the erstwhile 24 Pargana district, on the east by the Bongaon subdivision of the erstwhile Jessore district of undivided Bengal, and on the north by the Krishnagar Subdivision. The head-quarters of the subdivision originally used to be at Santipur, but it was transferred to Ranaghat in the year 1863. The subdivision was divided, for police purposes into three thanas, viz., Ranaghat, Santipur, and Chakdaha.

What the SDO Office Does

The SDO acts as the administrative head of Ranaghat subdivision, under the Nadia district collectorate and covers duties including:

Land revenue collection, mutation, leased land distribution
Disaster and emergency management oversight
Supervision of block level offices and administrative units

Our Services

Key Functions of the SDO, Ranaghat

General Administration
  • Organizes public grievance redressal meetings, public hearings, and inspections.
Magisterial Powers
  • Conducts inquiries into unnatural deaths, riots, unlawful assemblies, and other law-and-order matters.
Revenue Administration
  • Ensures maintenance of government land records and protection of government properties.
Development Monitoring
  • Monitors the functioning of municipality, panchayats, SHGs, and NGOs involved in government programs
Disaster Management
  • Acts as the nodal officer for disaster preparedness and response (floods, cyclones, etc.).
Law & Order Coordination
  • Coordinates with the Sub-Divisional Police Officer (SDPO) for maintaining law and order.
