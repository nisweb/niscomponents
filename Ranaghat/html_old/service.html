Our Services

Key Functions of the SDO, Ranaghat

General Administration
  • Supervises and coordinates the activities of all block offices (Ranaghat I, Ranaghat II, Santipur, Hanskhali).
  • Maintains law and order in coordination with the police.
  • Organizes public grievance redressal meetings, public hearings, and inspections.
Magisterial Powers
  • Exercises Executive Magistrate powers under the CrPC.
  • Conducts inquiries into unnatural deaths, riots, unlawful assemblies, and other law-and-order matters.
  • Issues curfew orders, prohibitory orders (Section 144) as needed.
  • Monitors election processes at the subdivision level (e.g., Assembly, Panchayat, Lok Sabha).
Revenue Administration
  • Oversees land records, mutation, land distribution, and land reform implementation through the SDL & LRO.
  • Supervises revenue collection and disposal of land dispute cases.
  • Ensures maintenance of government land records and protection of government properties.
Development Monitoring
  • Coordinates implementation of all major government welfare schemes and development projects (MGNREGA, PMAY, Jal Jeevan Mission, etc.).
  • Reviews progress of schemes with BDOs and other officials.
  • Monitors the functioning of municipality, panchayats, SHGs, and NGOs involved in government programs
Disaster Management
  • Acts as the nodal officer for disaster preparedness and response (floods, cyclones, etc.).
  • Coordinates with Civil Defence, police, health, PWD, irrigation, and other departments.
  • Manages relief distribution and shelter operations.
Law & Order Coordination
  • Coordinates with the Sub-Divisional Police Officer (SDPO) for maintaining law and order.
  • Engages with local intelligence units, especially during festivals, elections, or unrest.
Liaison Officer for Other Departments
  • Acts as the link between the district headquarters (Krishnanagar) and local line departments such as:
    • Education (DI/School)
    • Agriculture (ADA)
    • Food & Supplies
    • Health (BMOH/CMOH)
    • Social Welfare & BCW
Public Services Delivery
  • Supervises Duare Sarkar camps, public service delivery under citizen charters, issuance of:
    • Caste, Income, Residential certificates
    • Kanyashree, Rupashree verifications
    • e-district services
  • Redressal of public grievances through SDO office
