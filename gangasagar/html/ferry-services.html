Bus Services to Gangasagar

Bus services provide an economical and convenient mode of transportation for pilgrims and visitors travelling to Gangasagar. Regular bus connectivity is available from Kolkata towards Kakdwip and Harwood Point (Lot No. 8), the main ferry boarding point for reaching Sagar Island.

Travellers can board buses from major transport locations in Kolkata, including Esplanade and Babughat. After reaching Harwood Point, passengers need to cross the Muriganga River by ferry to Kachuberia. From Kachuberia, local bus services and other transport facilities are available to reach Gangasagar.

Major Bus Routes

Explore the available road transport connections.

Kolkata to Kakdwip

Bus services connect Kolkata with Kakdwip, providing access to Harwood Point (Lot No. 8) for ferry transportation.

Kolkata to Harwood Point

Pilgrims can travel towards Harwood Point (Lot No. 8), the major ferry terminal connecting the mainland with Sagar Island.

Kachuberia to Gangasagar

Local bus services and other shared transport facilities connect Kachuberia with Gangasagar and nearby destinations.

Journey to Gangasagar by Bus

Follow these simple steps to reach Gangasagar.

1
Kolkata

Start your journey from Esplanade or Babughat by bus towards Kakdwip.

2
Harwood Point

Reach Lot No. 8 at Harwood Point, Kakdwip, for ferry transportation.

3
Kachuberia

Cross the Muriganga River by ferry and reach Kachuberia on Sagar Island.

4
Gangasagar

Board a local bus or shared vehicle to reach Kapil Muni Ashram and the beach.

Local Bus Services on Sagar Island

Local bus transportation plays an important role in connecting different parts of Sagar Island. Pilgrims can use available bus services and shared vehicles to travel from Kachuberia towards Gangasagar.

These transport facilities help visitors reach important locations, including Kapil Muni Ashram, Gangasagar Mela Ground and nearby areas.

  • Connectivity from Kachuberia
  • Local transportation facilities
  • Shared and public transport options
  • Additional transport arrangements during Mela

Important Bus Boarding Points

Esplanade

One of the major transport hubs in Kolkata from where travellers can look for bus services towards Kakdwip.

Babughat

An important transport point for pilgrims travelling from Kolkata towards the Gangasagar route.

Kakdwip

The main mainland connection for reaching Harwood Point and boarding ferries to Kachuberia.

Important Travel Information

  • Check the latest bus timings and availability before starting your journey.
  • Bus services and routes may vary depending on the season and operating arrangements.
  • Ferry services from Harwood Point to Kachuberia are subject to tide and weather conditions.
  • During Gangasagar Mela, additional bus and transport arrangements may be introduced for the convenience of pilgrims.
  • Passengers are advised to start early and allow sufficient time for ferry transfers.
