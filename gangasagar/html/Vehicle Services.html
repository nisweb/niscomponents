Vehicle Transportation at Gangasagar

Vehicle transportation plays an important role in connecting pilgrims and visitors with different destinations across Sagar Island. A range of transportation options, including private cars, taxis, local buses, shared vehicles and electric rickshaws, helps visitors travel conveniently.

Visitors travelling from Kolkata can reach Harwood Point (Lot No. 8), Kakdwip, by private vehicle. From there, passengers can cross the Muriganga River by ferry to Kachuberia and continue towards Gangasagar using local transportation.

Special vehicle movement, parking and traffic management arrangements may also be introduced during Gangasagar Mela to facilitate the smooth movement of pilgrims.

Local Transportation on Sagar Island

Local transportation services are available to help visitors travel between Kachuberia, Gangasagar Bus Stand, Kapil Muni Ashram, Snan Ghat and nearby locations.

Depending on availability, passengers can choose from local buses, shared vehicles, taxis, trekkers and electric rickshaws.

  • Kachuberia to Gangasagar connectivity
  • Local sightseeing transportation
  • Hotel and accommodation transfers
  • Short-distance passenger services

Vehicle Parking Facilities

Visitors travelling by private vehicles should use designated parking areas near the ferry terminals and other authorised locations.

During Gangasagar Mela, special traffic regulations, vehicle restrictions and designated parking arrangements may be implemented by the administration to manage large-scale pilgrim movement.

  • Designated parking areas
  • Traffic management arrangements
  • Vehicle entry regulations
  • Follow authorised parking instructions

Vehicle Ferry & LCT Services

Vehicle-carrying vessels, including Landing Craft Tank (LCT) and other authorised arrangements, may facilitate the movement of eligible vehicles across the Muriganga River between Lot No. 8 and Kachuberia.

Vehicle transportation is subject to vessel availability, vehicle dimensions, loading capacity, tide conditions and operational permissions.

  • Vehicle transportation subject to availability
  • Authorised vehicle boarding facilities
  • Vehicle document verification
  • Follow ferry terminal instructions

Vehicle Management During Gangasagar Mela

During Gangasagar Mela, a large number of vehicles arrive from different parts of the state to facilitate the movement of pilgrims. The administration may introduce special traffic management systems, designated parking zones, vehicle entry restrictions and transport arrangements.

These arrangements help regulate traffic flow, reduce congestion and ensure the safe movement of pilgrims and emergency service vehicles.

Traffic Management

Organised vehicle movement and traffic regulation at important locations.

Parking Management

Designated vehicle parking arrangements for pilgrims and visitors.

Emergency Vehicles

Priority movement arrangements for ambulances and other emergency response vehicles.

Important Travel Guidelines

  • Check vehicle entry restrictions and traffic advisories before starting your journey.
  • Park vehicles only in designated and authorised parking areas.
  • Carry valid driving licence, vehicle registration certificate and insurance documents.
  • Confirm vehicle ferry availability and applicable charges in advance.
  • Follow traffic signs, police instructions and local administrative guidelines.
  • Allow additional travel time during Gangasagar Mela and peak pilgrimage periods.
