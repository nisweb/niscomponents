How to Reach Gangasagar

Sagardwip, popularly known as Gangasagar, is located approximately 123 km from Kolkata. It is one of the most sacred pilgrimage destinations in West Bengal. Visitors can reach Gangasagar through a combination of air, road, railway and waterways. Whether you are travelling from Kolkata or other parts of India, several convenient transport options are available to reach this beautiful island.

Ways to Reach Gangasagar

By Air

You can take a flight to Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata, which is approximately 142 km from the island.

From the airport, take a taxi or bus towards Kakdwip. From there, complete the remaining journey by ferry to reach Gangasagar.

Nearest Airport: Kolkata
View Airport Location

By Road

Visitors can take a bus from Babughat or Esplanade Bus Terminus in Kolkata towards Kakdwip. The road journey takes approximately 3 hours.

From Kakdwip, cross the Muriganga River by ferry and reach Kachuberia. From Kachuberia, buses and other local transport are available to reach Gangasagar.

Route: Kolkata – Kakdwip – Kachuberia – Gangasagar
View Road Route

By Train

You can travel by train from Sealdah Railway Station to Kakdwip or Namkhana Railway Station.

From either station, you will need to arrange local transport and ferry services to complete your journey to Sagardwip.

Nearest Stations: Kakdwip / Namkhana
View Railway Location

Ferry & Local Transportation

Complete your journey with convenient local transportation services.

Ferry Services

Ferry services connect Kakdwip with Kachuberia across the Muriganga River. This waterway is an essential part of the journey to Gangasagar.

Local Transport

Public transport is one of the convenient ways to travel around Sagardwip. Buses and other local transport services are available for pilgrims and visitors.
