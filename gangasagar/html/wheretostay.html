Accommodation at Gangasagar

Pilgrims visiting the Gangasagar Mela can avail of comfortable lodging facilities at various locations across the region. Several pilgrim sheds have been arranged at important locations including Lot-8, Kachuberia, Sagar, Benuban, Chemaguri and Namkhana to facilitate the accommodation of devotees. Visitors can find accommodation details according to their preferred locations below.
