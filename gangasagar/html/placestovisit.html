Explore Gangasagar

Discover sacred temples, peaceful beaches and remarkable attractions. Experience the spiritual and natural beauty of this beautiful destination.

The Sagar Beach

Located at the southern tip of Sagardwip, Sagar Beach is one of the most popular attractions of Gangasagar. Every year, thousands of pilgrims visit this beach for a holy dip at the sacred confluence of the River Ganga and the Bay of Bengal. It is also a peaceful place to enjoy beautiful sunsets and the soothing sea breeze.

Gangasagar Pilgrimage & Fair

Gangasagar is a place of immense religious and cultural significance. Situated at the meeting point of the River Ganga and the Bay of Bengal, it hosts the famous Gangasagar Mela every year in January. Thousands of devotees from across the country gather here to take a holy dip and offer their prayers.

Kapil Muni Temple

Dedicated to the revered sage Kapil Muni, this temple is the principal religious attraction of Sagardwip. After taking a holy dip, pilgrims visit the temple to offer prayers. According to Hindu mythology, Sage Kapil Muni is associated with the sacred legend of King Sagar and the descent of the River Ganga.

The Sagar Lighthouse

The Sagar Lighthouse is an interesting landmark near the beach at Sagardwip. It offers beautiful views of the surrounding coastal landscape and the sea. It is also an attractive spot for visitors who enjoy photography and exploring the natural beauty of the island.

Bharat Seva Ashram

Bharat Seva Ashram is a well-known spiritual institution in Gangasagar. The ashram has a temple and provides accommodation facilities for visitors and pilgrims. It is a peaceful place where devotees can spend time in prayer and spiritual activities.

Onkarnath Temple

Onkarnath Temple is another sacred spiritual destination in Gangasagar, dedicated to Lord Onkar. Surrounded by greenery and a peaceful natural environment, the temple offers visitors a calm atmosphere for prayer, meditation and spiritual reflection.

Nearby Attractions

Explore some of the beautiful coastal and natural destinations around Gangasagar.

Bakkhali

Bakkhali is a beautiful crescent-shaped beach town in the southern part of West Bengal. Its long stretch of sandy beach offers spectacular sunrise and sunset views. Away from the busy city atmosphere, Bakkhali is an ideal destination for relaxation and enjoying nature. The nearby Bishhalakshmi Temple is another attraction for visitors.

Sajnekhali

Sajnekhali is one of the popular destinations in the Sundarbans and an important gateway for exploring its natural beauty. The Sajnekhali Watchtower offers panoramic views of the surrounding mangrove forests. Visitors can also explore the Nature Interpretation Centre, Bono Bibi Temple and Crocodile Park. The area is well known for its rich wildlife and birdwatching opportunities.
