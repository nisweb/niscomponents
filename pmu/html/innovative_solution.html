Tatkal Service for Caste Certificates

Caste certificates are essential for accessing various social benefits, government schemes, and opportunities in employment and education. However, delays in issuance often deprive eligible beneficiaries of timely access to their entitlements. A recent Backward Class Survey conducted by PMU across 200 villages in 22 districts, covering 5,059 households, revealed that only 41% of families have caste certificates for all members. Among these, 67% receive certificates within one month, while 15% wait for 2–3 months, 11% for 4–6 months, and 8% for over six months. Such delays result in eligible beneficiaries missing opportunities or facing significant hardships.

1. Existing System:

Applicants apply for caste certificate through online portal and submit the hardcopy of applications along with other documents to the block end. The Block level officials will neither be able to process nor be able to view the application till submission of the hardcopy by the applicant. Sometimes this is also causing delay in service delivery. The applicant either has to appear for hearing at the block office with all relevant original documents or a thorough enquiry has been conducted on case-to-case basis. Then it gets forwarded to the end of the concerned Sub Divisional Officer. Approval of the SDO through his DSC, would enable the applicant to download the certificate from the portal.

2. Proposed System:

On account of admission to the Educational Institutions, Job Interviews and other such cases where caste certificate requires to produce within a given timeframe, the candidates may apply through Tatkal option of the existing portal. This Tatkal option of the caste certificate portal may be routed through BSKs.

Tatkal tab in existing Portal: A dedicated mechanism prioritizes urgent applications.

Time-Bound Process: Tatkal applications may be processed within 7 working days.

Transparency and Accountability: Actions at each stage may be documented and visible to applicants through the portal.

Real-Time Tracking: Applicants receive SMS updates or can check the status online using the application number.

Hierarchical Escalation Mechanism: Defined stages ensure smooth and swift movement of applications.

3. Recommendations for Effective Implementation

1. Capacity Building:

Conduct training programs for SDOs, BDOs, enquiry officers, and BSK DEOs to handle tatkal cases efficiently.

2. Monitoring and Audits:

Establish a review mechanism to ensure adherence to timelines and minimize bureaucratic hurdles.

3. Technological Enhancements:

Introduce an integrated dashboard for real-time application monitoring by authorities

4. IEC Campaigns:

Disseminate information about the tatkal process and documentation requirements through community centers and local media to minimize incomplete submissions.
