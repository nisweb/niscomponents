Objectives of the PMU

The key objectives of the TSU will be to support the PMU in the following:

Assess the Effectiveness
Measure the outcomes of each welfare scheme against its stated objectives.
Analyze the Efficiency
Evaluate how efficiently the resources (financial, human, infrastructure) are being utilized.
Identify Key Challenges / Gaps
Highlight gaps and challenges in delivery and accessibility of these schemes at the grassroots level.

Scope of Work

Evidence, Research and Data Analytics

Policy analysis, advisory and coordination

Strengthening Governance and Institutional Mechanisms

Strategic Communication and Citizen Outreach
