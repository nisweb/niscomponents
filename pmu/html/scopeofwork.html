Scope of Work

1. Evidence, Research and Data Analytics

Shortlisting of Welfare Schemes:

The first step will be to compile a comprehensive list of existing welfare schemes in the State. This will involve a detailed review of the programs, highlighting those with the greatest impact and those needing enhancement. This shortlist will lay the groundwork for the monitoring and evidence generation exercises.

Analysing the current Social Welfare Schemes:

Desk review and evaluation of existing policies and programmes at state level. Provide policy inputs for preparation/updating of rules, guidelines, SOPs, action plans, directives, etc. for various schemes/sub-schemes.

Establish robust evidence generation methodologies:

The TSU team will design robust survey methodology and sampling strategies to undertake primary evidence and periodic assessments using large-scale rapid surveys (pulse surveys, perception surveys, telephonic surveys and citizen welfare delivery survey) for assessing the status of various social protection schemes across different demographic groups

Development of Monitoring Tools:

Based on insights from the stakeholders, a set of tools will be developed to gather feedback from beneficiaries. These tools will be user-friendly, accessible, and capable of capturing qualitative and quantitative data. They will be designed to assess the effectiveness of the schemes and identify areas for improvement.

Discussion with Key Interest Groups and Stakeholders:

Engaging with the stakeholders, including government officials, elected representatives, district administration and PRI functionaries will be crucial in understanding the perspectives, expectations, and experiences of the State government and it officials with regards to the welfare schemes. Stakeholder input will inform the development of monitoring tools and the overall strategy.

Training and Capacity Building of Data Collection team:

The TSU team will work with the PMU to guide and strengthen capacity of the field data collection team on survey instruments and tools, sampling methodologies, communication protocols, etc. The data collection team will be hired separately by the Government as per the requirements.

Monitoring the data collection:

Data will be collected through field visits using various methods such as digital forms, in-depth interviews (IDIs), surveys and direct observations. The TSU team will work on developing digital tools/forms for data collection. The data will be stored on the server of the state government. During data collection, TSU team members may also visit in select locations to monitor data collection and obtaining feedback from select beneficiaries.

Recurrent monitoring and field visits:

To assess changes in beneficiary satisfaction following the implementation of recommendations from the previous field visit. This process involves systematically evaluating the impact of implemented measures on beneficiary experiences and perceptions, ensuring continuous improvement in service delivery and effectiveness of welfare initiatives.

Data Analysis:

The collected data will be analysed to extract meaningful insights. The analysis will identify trends, success factors, and areas for improvement. It will also evaluate the impact of the schemes on the target population and capture overall perception of beneficiaries about governance and benefits of Government’s welfare schemes.

2. Policy analysis, advisory and coordination

Discussion of Results with Stakeholders:

The findings from the data analysis will be discussed with key stakeholders to help refine the findings and shape the actionable recommendations for the competent authorities.

Policy Memos and Reports:

Detailed monthly and quarterly reports will be compiled, summarizing the data, key analytics, and stakeholder discussions. The report will provide actionable insights and suggestions for improving the social welfare schemes.

Coordination and Collaboration:

Support the PMU in coordination with multiple government departments and the district administrations regarding the findings of the rapid pulse surveys to ensure cohesive and effective implementation of various schemes across areas lagging behind in service delivery and assist the PMU to conduct regular follow up of implementation of recommendations

Welfare service delivery and governance outcomes priority matrix :

Develop a matrix with key priority indicators based on factors such as public need, resource availability, and strategic importance focused on key interest groups and stakeholders with focused timelines.

Identification of the best practices across States:

To identify and benchmark other best practices from different States and customizing those practices according to the State’s need and priority

3. Strengthening Governance and Institutional Mechanisms

Improved calibration of schedules of work with district administration:

Ensure improved coordination with the district administration to streamline schedules and workflows to enhance the timely execution of welfare schemes and foster consistent communication between the state-level officials and the district administration

Strengthen effective implementation modalities:

Work in close coordination with various state government departments by creating unified processes to refine and streamline implementation modalities and ensure convergence in execution across various departments.

Assessment of institutional processes and strategic road map for its enhancement:

Undertake a thorough assessment of institutional processes within the current governance frameworks to identify gaps and develop a strategic roadmap to enhance coordination, implementation and improved last-mile service delivery

Develop case studies to highlight improved governance outcomes:

Develop knowledge management outputs and comprehensive case studies documenting and highlighting improved governance outcomes achieved through PMU interventions, showcasing best practices and lessons for broader application across the state.

4. Strategic Communication and Citizen Outreach

Review and strengthen IEC Campaigns:

Support the PMU in analysing, monitoring and review of existing IEC and public awareness campaigns of the priority welfare schemes and help strengthen a robust IEC framework for the state government

360 degree digital and traditional media campaign:

Support the PMU to formulate tailored digital and traditional media-based communication campaigns including mass media, social media, and community outreach programmes in close collaboration/ coordination with relevant government departments implementing priority schemes.

Dedicated PMU Social Media Channel:

Support the PMU to help set up a dedicated Social Media Channel for the PMU to enable receipt of citizen feedback, track sentiment analysis and geo-spatial analysis of key policy and socio-economic issues, monitor public sentiment, identify trends, and respond quickly to service delivery issues

Targeted citizen outreach and dissemination:

Support the PMU to undertake a targeted periodic citizen outreach and dissemination campaign on public service delivery through use of communication tools including WhatsApp, SMS, IVRS and Outdoor Campaigns.

PMU

West Bengal, with a population of about 9.13 Crore residents, is the fourth most populous state in the country. Around 67% of the population of the State resides in rural areas, while 33% resides in urban area. Women make up a significant proportion, totalling 4.45 crore, accounting for 48.7% of the state's total population. Children constitute about 1.06 crore, nearly 12% of the overall populace. Additionally, West Bengal is home to 2.14 crore individuals from Scheduled Castes and 0.53 Crore from Scheduled Tribes, with women forming a substantial part of these communities. The youth population of the State aged 15-29 years is about 2.58 Crore which is almost 28.26% of the total population of the State. The Government of West Bengal is dedicated towards social justice and inclusive growth through proactive social welfare schemes aimed at enhancing the lives of women, children, youth and other vulnerable groups.
