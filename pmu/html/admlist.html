PMU

West Bengal, with a population of about 9.13 Crore residents, is the fourth most populous state in the country. Around 67% of the population of the State resides in rural areas, while 33% resides in urban area. Women make up a significant proportion, totalling 4.45 crore, accounting for 48.7% of the state's total population. Children constitute about 1.06 crore, nearly 12% of the overall populace. Additionally, West Bengal is home to 2.14 crore individuals from Scheduled Castes and 0.53 Crore from Scheduled Tribes, with women forming a substantial part of these communities. The youth population of the State aged 15-29 years is about 2.58 Crore which is almost 28.26% of the total population of the State. The Government of West Bengal is dedicated towards social justice and inclusive growth through proactive social welfare schemes aimed at enhancing the lives of women, children, youth and other vulnerable groups.
