Key Features

Samriddhi POS is equipped with powerful features designed to simplify retail operations, improve efficiency,

Fast & Accurate Billing

Generate invoices quickly with an easy-to-use billing interface. Support barcode scanning, multiple payment methods, discounts, and tax calculations to ensure faster and error-free checkout.

Inventory Management

Keep track of your stock in real time with automatic inventory updates. Monitor product availability, receive low-stock alerts, and efficiently manage purchases and stock movements.

Customer Management

Store customer information, purchase history, and contact details in one place. Build stronger customer relationships through loyalty programs and personalized services.

Sales & Business Reports

Access detailed reports on sales, inventory, profits, and customer activities. Make informed business decisions with real-time analytics and comprehensive performance insights.

Barcode Integration

Speed up billing and inventory operations with seamless barcode scanner support. Easily identify products and reduce manual data entry for improved accuracy.

Multi-Payment Support

Accept payments through cash, debit/credit cards, UPI, mobile wallets, and other digital payment methods, providing customers with a smooth and flexible checkout experience.

User & Role Management

Create multiple user accounts with customized roles and permissions. Secure sensitive business information while allowing employees access based on their responsibilities.

Secure Data Backup

Protect your valuable business data with automatic backups and secure storage. Easily recover information whenever needed, ensuring business continuity and peace of mind.
