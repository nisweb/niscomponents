General FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Samriddhi POS

1. What is Samriddhi POS? +

Samriddhi POS is a Point of Sale (POS) system designed for retail businesses. It helps you manage billing, inventory, customer data, and generate reports efficiently. It's specifically tailored for small to medium-sized retail shops.

2. How do I log in to Samriddhi POS? +
  • Open the app.
  • Enter your registered mobile number.
  • Enter your password.
  • Click the "Login" button.
  • If you're a cashier, you'll be redirected to the billing screen.
3. I forgot my password. What should I do? +
  • On the login screen, click "Forgot Password".
  • Enter your registered mobile number.
  • You'll receive an OTP on your registered mobile.
  • Enter the OTP and set a new password.
  • Login with your new password.
4. How do I select a shop? +
  • Login to the app.
  • You'll see a list of shops assigned to your account.
  • Tap on the shop you want to work with.
  • Confirm your selection.
  • You'll be redirected to the billing screen.
